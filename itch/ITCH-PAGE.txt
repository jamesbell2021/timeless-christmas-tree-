DESCRIPTION (paste into the Description box)
--------------------------------------------
Christmas Eve, 1901. A fir tree that began as a seed beneath the Mourne Mountains has been carried home through the snow, and it is standing in the parlour, waiting for you.

A Timeless Christmas Tree is a gentle Christmas game with no timers, no scores and nothing to get wrong. It plays in your browser, on a computer, tablet or phone.

FIRST, A SHORT STORY
Four hand-painted chapters follow the tree from a seedling in spring 1893, through eight years of sun, gales and snow, to the lantern-lit evening a family chooses it. A robin keeps it company the whole way. You can skip the story, or watch any chapter again later.

THEN, THE PARLOUR IS YOURS
Open the old trunk and you will find 54 period decorations in six velvet trays:
- beeswax candles and the very first electric fairy lamps
- blown glass from Lauscha, Dresden paper stars, gingerbread and clove oranges
- tinsel, popcorn, cranberry and glass-bead garlands that you drape yourself
- four tree toppers
- stockings for the mantel, parcels, a teddy bear, a rocking horse, a wreath and mistletoe

Drag them where you like. Sweep the pointer through the branches and the ornaments swing and chime. Light the candles one by one, dim the lamps, and watch the glass catch the light.

FIRESIDE MODE: A CHRISTMAS SCREENSAVER
When your tree is finished, press F (or the little hearth button). The buttons disappear, the lamps go down, and the view drifts slowly around the room while the fire crackles, the snow falls and the carols play. If you have decorated more than one tree, it turns to the next one every few minutes. Put it on the big screen and leave it on all evening. Click or press any key to come back.

ALSO IN THE BOX
- An Album with six pages, so you can keep six different trees
- Share Your Design: turns your parlour into a vintage Christmas card you can download and send
- Five carols played on a soft piano and harp: O Tannenbaum, Silent Night, Good King Wenceslas, Deck the Hall and We Wish You a Merry Christmas
- Everything saves by itself in your browser

MADE ENTIRELY IN CODE
There is not a single image or sound file in this game. Every picture, every carol, the crackle of the fire and the chime of the glass is generated by code as you play.

CONTROLS
- Open the trunk: the Open the Trunk button, or T
- Place or move a decoration: drag it
- Put one away: right-click it, or drag it back to the trunk
- Light the tree / the lamps: C / L
- Fireside mode (screensaver): F
- Hide the buttons: H
- Undo: Ctrl+Z
- Music on or off: M

Tip: press the fullscreen button under the game for the best view.
